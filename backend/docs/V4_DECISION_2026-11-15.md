## What the two sides actually measure

| Input | PGDA 69 reference (source) | V4 tool / runtime | Comparable? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Visibility rule | Any part of the disc unobscured (product page; PDS label `AVGVISIB_85S_060M_201608.LBL`) | Body centre above the skyline (PRD runtime rule) | **No** |
| Averaging | Hourly time steps over 18.6 years (product page) | Daily at 00:00 UTC over 18.6 years from 2000-01-01 | Approximately; daily is noisier, not biased |
| Observer height | Not published; the page notes that height above the surface needs further analysis | 0 m (`validate_illumination.py`) | Assumed, not verified |
| Terrain | LDEM_85S_060M (60 m), DE421 era | PGDA 90 20 m near + 160 m far | No; second-order |
| Ephemeris | DE421 era | SPICE DE440 | Negligible at this scale |

Source checked 15 Nov 2026: https://pgda.gsfc.nasa.gov/products/69 ("averaged over hourly
timesteps spanning 18.6 years"; the Earth is "considered visible if any part of its disc is not
obscured"). The solar PDS label uses the same any-fraction rule (see `V4_SOURCE_REVIEW.md`).

The rule mismatch has the observed sign. A centre rule can never count more visibility than an
any-fraction rule, and the runtime is lower (7.8% < 14.1%). The recorded diagnostic
(`V4-disc-sensitivity.json`) adds a fixed 0.27° solar radius and closes 2.28 pp. That does not
pass, and a fixed radius is not a full disc model.

## Proposed like-for-like validation (historical proposal; decided — see the decision record)

**"V4b: any-fraction disc visibility"**, validation-only (the runtime keeps its approved
centre rule):

- **Same 5 points**, **≤ 5 pp target**, **18.6 years from 2000-01-01**, **hourly** steps (the
  reference's sampling).
- **Visible** when the upper limb clears the skyline. The disc's angular radius is computed per
  sample from SPICE distance. The skyline is taken as the maximum across the azimuths the disc
  spans, which is conservative.
- **Report both numbers:** V4 (centre) stays FAIL as a recorded mismatch, and V4b is judged on
  its own.
- **Cost:** about 24 times the current daily run (≈ 163 k samples per point). Run it offline
  and record it with the existing evidence schema.

**Decision that was required (historical; decided 15 Nov 2026 — see the decision record):**

1. Accept V4b as the acceptance basis for the PRD V4 item, with V4 kept as documented FAIL.
2. Or keep V4 as the sole criterion and ship with a known V4 FAIL (disclosed in Methods).

Methods and release notes still say V4 FAILS, with this explanation, alongside the adopted V4b PASS. V6 keeps its
qualitative owner-reviewed status; it is not independent certification.

## Reference conventions checked (V4b side session, 15 Nov 2026)

**Verified:**

- **Solar rule.** The PDS label (`AVGVISIB_85S_060M_201608.LBL`) counts a pixel when "sunlit
  (by any fraction of the solar disc)".
- **Earth rule.** The PGDA 69 page counts Earth as visible "if any part of its disc is not
  obscured".
- **Sampling.** "averaged over hourly timesteps spanning 18.6 years".
- **Terrain.** "based on the LOLA elevation map LDEM_85S_060M" (60 m), frame "MEAN EARTH/POLAR
  AXIS OF DE421".

**Not published, so assumed:**

- **Averaging start epoch.** V4b uses 2000-01-01. The label's START_TIME/STOP_TIME
  (2009–2013) are LOLA acquisition dates, not the simulation interval.
- **Observer height.** V4b uses 0 m; the page notes that height above the surface needs
  "further analysis".
- **Ephemeris version.** V4b uses SPICE DE440; the reference frame is DE421-based.

These remaining mismatches (epoch, height, DEM, ephemeris) are why V4b is a like-for-like
*definition* check and not an exact reproduction. The 4.1 pp Sun row at (−88, 90 E) is the
largest residual.
